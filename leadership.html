Identity & Self-Discovery

The programme opened with 59 participants reflecting deeply on who they are and who they want to become. Girls shared personal aspirations and connected directly with key African Girl Guide leaders.

This first week laid the foundation for everything that follows: purpose-driven leadership that starts with knowing yourself and grows into impact on your community.

Study visit · April 8, 2026 · Abuja

Integrity, up close at the EFCC.

African Girl Guide delegates visited the headquarters of Nigeria’s Economic and Financial Crimes Commission in Abuja. The session focused on integrity, accountability and cybercrime awareness, with a clear message to the girls: stay true to yourselves and do the right thing.

Visits like this show participants how national institutions work, and the kind of leaders those institutions need.
