Our story

Compassion that crossed an ocean and came home to her.

African Girl Guide is the African outreach of Paw Salvation, a Chicago-based 501(c)(3) nonprofit with more than eight years of community impact in the United States.

The founder

A dream carried across an ocean and brought home.

Jindu Elueze arrived in the United States from Nigeria with little more than hope and resolve. In Chicago he founded Paw Salvation, which grew from feeding the pets of struggling families into a community movement of rescue, outreach and education, serving veterans, seniors and neighbours experiencing homelessness, and treating service as a shared responsibility.

African Girl Guide and the Paw Salvation Moving Library carry that same compassion to West Africa. They are the two flagship initiatives of its international mission, now officially launched in Nigeria and Ghana.
