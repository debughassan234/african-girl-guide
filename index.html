Why we exist

When a girl stays in school and finds a mentor, a family changes. Then a community. Then a nation.

The barriers she faces

Family finances that push girls out of school. No mentor, no career guidance. Gender norms that cap ambition. Too few books and working classrooms. Hunger that makes learning harder. Exposure to exploitation.

How we answer

Dignity, education and sustainability, together. Communities shape the work with us. Impact is tracked with data. Schools, governments and companies partner in it. We build independence, never dependency, and we show every girl the world is bigger than her circumstances.

What we measure

School retention for girls. Literacy in rural communities. Leadership and life skills. Access to digital skills and global opportunity. And, over time, lasting social mobility.

Three pillars. One girl at the centre.

Every programme we run strengthens at least one of these, and the strongest ones do all three.

Leadership Series

A 12-week Leadership Exposure Programme that moves from identity and self-discovery to purpose-driven leadership and community impact, with weekly highlights shared publicly.

  • Weekly sessions and reflections with AGG leaders
  • Study visits to national institutions, including the EFCC in Abuja
  • A peer network that lasts beyond the 12 weeks

Paw Salvation Moving Library

The Moving Library

A mobile classroom that brings learning to children where schools are scarce, pairing literacy with nutrition and emotional support, because a hungry child cannot focus.

  • Books, learning materials, tutoring and structured lessons
  • Social-emotional learning in a safe space
  • Nutritious snacks to help concentration

Her path with us.

Support that follows a girl from the classroom to her first leadership role and beyond.

  1. 1
    Stay.

    Fees, supplies and transport covered so she never has to choose between school and survival.

  2. 2
    Belong.

    Matched with a mentor who guides her academically, emotionally and professionally.

  3. 3
    Lead.

    The 12-week Leadership Series, study visits to national institutions, and a peer network.

  4. 4
    Rise.

    Internships, earning opportunities and, soon, seed capital to build what’s next.
